SakuRapi — Account Deletion Policy

Effective Date: October 12, 2026

At SakuRapi, we take your privacy seriously and are committed to giving you full control over your personal data. This Account and Data Deletion Policy explains how you can request the deletion of your account and the personal information associated with it.

1. How to Request Account Deletion (In-App)

We provide a straightforward way for you to delete your account and all associated data directly from within the SakuRapi app. To proceed with account deletion, please follow these steps:

  1. Open the SakuRapi application and log in to your account.
  2. Navigate to the Profile or Settings menu.
  3. Scroll down and tap on the Delete Account option.
  4. Follow the on-screen prompts to confirm your decision.

2. How to Request Account Deletion (Via Email)

If you no longer have access to the SakuRapi application or encounter any issues with the in-app deletion process, you can request account deletion manually by contacting our support team:

  1. Send an email to dev.sakurapi@gmail.com.
  2. Use the subject line: "SakuRapi Account Deletion Request".
  3. Ensure you send the email from the exact email address associated with your SakuRapi account so we can verify your identity.
  4. Include a brief statement confirming that you want your account and all associated data permanently deleted.

3. What Data Will Be Deleted?

When you confirm your account deletion, we will permanently remove the following information from our active databases:

  • Personal Information: Your email address, password, and Google OAuth authentication links.
  • Financial & App Data: Your transaction history, budget records, parsed receipts, custom UI themes, and any other personalized settings.

4. Data Retention Period

  • Immediate Deletion: Once your request is confirmed, your account credentials and personal information are immediately deleted from our active servers.
  • Backups: For security and system stability, encrypted backups may retain some data for up to 30 days. After this period, all traces of your data will be permanently purged.
  • Exceptions: We do not retain any of your personal data unless strictly required by law to comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, or enforce our agreements.

If you have any questions about this policy, please contact us at dev.sakurapi@gmail.com.
